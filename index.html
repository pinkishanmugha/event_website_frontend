About VyomX

VyomX is the flagship event of the Space Club of LBS Institute of Technology for Women, bringing together students, researchers and space enthusiasts to explore innovations in space science, satellite technology and interplanetary missions. Join us for an inspiring journey beyond Earth through expert talks, workshops and exhibitions.
